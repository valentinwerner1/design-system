03 / Menu and sort

Our menu, not the platform's

hindsight and loquol both replaced native selects because GTK drew them white-on-white. The chosen item gets a tick; the keyboard position gets a wash. Arrow keys work. Rectangle or pill: by where it sits

12 / Pipeline

Stages of readiness

One icon per stage; the colour is the state, so there is no tick. Green on a green tint is done, faint is pending, and a stage you marked done by hand keeps a dashed outline, so it never passes for one that was actually met. Hover names the stage.

13 / Table

Short columns shrink, long text gets the room

A column that doesn't need width doesn't get it, so the long-text column stops wrapping early. Everything happens on the column name. Text columns search: the magnifier opens a narrow field over the header. Columns with a few repeating values get a funnel with checkboxes. Numbers and dates sort; a date gets a funnel only for presets that answer a real question ("Last 7 days"). Any active filter turns its icon yellow. Long sequences are tables, not chip soup.

Honest graphs

21 / Scrollbars

Calm chrome

Thin, no track, no arrows; dimmer and quieter than the browser's, never the lightest thing on screen.

hindsight scrollbars

Scroll me. Pre-training, fine-tuning and RLHF, in four modules. Each module is 15 to 30 minutes and ends with a short check. Topics in your areas count towards your progress; everything else stays browsable. News never undoes your progress. Modules that changed since you passed them come back under Waiting on you. A short test ticks them off again. Ratings from the self-assessment never count; passing a test does.

22 / Touch targets

Room for a finger

A touch target is the area that reacts to a tap, which can be bigger than what you see. A mouse pointer hits a 34px icon button easily; a fingertip covers about 10mm and misses. On a phone (under 760px) every button and control grows its box to 44px, the size Apple and Google recommend. The icon stays the same, so nothing looks bigger, it's just easier to hit.
